Welcome to My Research Blog

I’m excited to launch this space to share my thoughts on research, data science, and the intersection of technology and policy.

What You’ll Find Here

I plan to write about:

  • Sustainable Computing & Green AI — The environmental impact of machine learning systems and strategies for more efficient computing
  • Data Governance — How institutions can responsibly manage collective data resources
  • Research Methods — Deep dives into causal inference, machine learning techniques, and statistical analysis
  • Policy & Technology — Observations on how governance can keep pace with rapid technological change
  • Global Perspectives — Insights from living and working across East Asia, Europe, and Africa

Why This Blog?

As a researcher working at the intersection of data science and public policy, I believe there’s value in sharing:

  • Work-in-progress research findings
  • Technical explanations of complex methods
  • Lessons learned from research projects
  • Reactions to new developments in AI, sustainability, and governance

What to Expect

  • Articles every 2-4 weeks (goal — no promises!)
  • Mix of technical and policy content — something for different audiences
  • Real research — not just polished papers, but the thinking process
  • Questions & uncertainty — I don’t have all the answers, and I’m genuinely exploring these topics
